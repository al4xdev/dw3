Digimon World 2003, the RK3326 port. Nothing of the game is in this folder.

Yours: game/ (the .cue and the .bin together), settings.json, pad.map, the two .mcd cards, logs/.
How to install, the menu and the controls are in README.md.

logs/game.log is the run. DW3_FORCE_VIDEO and DW3_FORCE_RENDERER override the launcher.
Select + Start quits.
